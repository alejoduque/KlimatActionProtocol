## What KLAP is

KLAP (Klimat Action Protocol) is a free tool for group decisions. Groups use it in climate campaigns and in stocktakes. A stocktake is a review of what a group did, what worked and what to do next.

## How it works

1. One person writes a claim.
2. Other people add reasons. Each reason has a type: **because** (for the claim), **but** (against the claim) or **however** (more information or a condition).
3. People support a reason, or they report an error in it (a fallacy).
4. KLAP calculates a weight for each reason. Then it shows the support rate or the objection rate of the claim.
5. The map shows all the reasons as a tree. The group sees where it agrees and where it does not agree.

In the map, the line style shows the type of reason: solid line = because, double line = but, dashed line = however.

## Permacomputing

KLAP follows permacomputing principles. It uses few resources and it does not track people. There are no accounts: each visitor gets an anonymous ID. KLAP uses system fonts, two colours and no analytics.

Reference: [Permacomputing Aesthetics](https://monoskop.org/images/6/6a/Mansoux_Aymeric_et_al_2023_Permacomputing_Aesthetics.pdf), Mansoux et al. (2023).
